Sound familiar?

  • People sign up and never get the confirmation email.
  • "Forgot password" does nothing, or you hit a 429 / "email rate limit exceeded" error.
  • The email arrives, but the link goes to localhost:3000 or your old preview address.
  • Everything works in the Lovable / Bolt / Vercel preview, but login is broken on your real domain.
  • "Sign in with Google" throws a redirect_uri_mismatch error.

Usually this isn't a bug in your app. It's settings. Supabase's built-in email sender is for testing only: it sends just 2 emails an hour, and only to people on your Supabase team (Supabase docs). Real users get nothing until you plug in your own email service and point a few DNS records and URLs at the right places. It's fiddly and easy to get slightly wrong.

What I fix for $49

  • Custom email sending (SMTP): set up a free-tier email service (usually Resend; Brevo, Postmark or Zoho ZeptoMail if they suit you better) and connect it to Supabase Auth.
  • DNS records so emails don't land in spam: SPF, DKIM and a starter DMARC record on your domain.
  • Site URL and redirect URLs pointed at your live site, with dev/preview addresses kept separate.
  • Email templates checked so links go to the right place ({{ .SiteURL }} vs {{ .RedirectTo }}).
  • Email rate limit raised to a sensible number for your app.
  • A real test: sign up, confirm, reset password, all to an outside Gmail inbox, while you watch.
  • A short write-up afterwards of what was changed and the old values, so anything can be undone.

FAQ

Do you need my Supabase password or API keys?

No, and please don't send them. You stay logged in and share your screen. If an email service gives you an SMTP password, you paste it into Supabase yourself. I never need to see it.

Will this cost me anything monthly?

Usually no. Resend's free plan covers 3,000 emails a month (max 100 a day); Brevo's free plan allows 300 a day; Zoho ZeptoMail gives one free credit of 10,000 emails, valid for a month; Postmark's free plan is 100 emails a month for testing. Their prices can change; we'll check them on the day and pick what fits. Your domain needs to be one you control.

I built my app with Lovable / Bolt / Base44 / Cursor. Can you still help?

Yes, as long as the backend is a Supabase project you can log in to. If your database is inside Lovable Cloud (not your own Supabase account), some settings may not be reachable. Tell me on the form and I'll say honestly whether I can help.

Do I need a custom domain?

For reliable email, yes. Email services want to send from a domain you own. If you don't have one yet, we can cover what to buy, but the domain cost is yours.

Will you touch my database or code?

Not for the $49 fix. It's auth settings, email and DNS only. The table-permissions add-on runs a few SQL statements, and we read them together before running them.
